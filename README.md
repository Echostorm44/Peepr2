This is the second Peepr, The first one used a VLC lib to do the video and it made the startup time really slow on most boxes and it was really hard to control the video size.  So nearly 2 years ago I started building MediaKernel which is basically FFMPEG in C#, pure C#, not calling C or C++ dlls, every codec in pure C#.  It was a slog but it was worth it.  This Peepr opens almost instantly because all my image and video libs are AOT compatible and everything plays nice together because it was made from the ground up to do so.  

Ok, enough flexing, Peepr is both an image viewer and a video player in one, it isn't made to replace vlc, what it is made for is when you want to browse through a folder with still images, animated gifs and short videos and not have to jump between a photo viewer and a video player.  I mean, it is still a really good image viewer on it's own and you could watch a movie with with it, you know what, you do you, if you want to watch the Lord of The Rings on Peepr, who am I to stop you. Go crazy.
